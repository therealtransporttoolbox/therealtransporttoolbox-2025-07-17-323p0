In May 2026, NHVR officers stopped a rigid truck towing a trailer at Twelve Mile Creek. The work diary said the driver was resting. Road safety cameras had seen the truck driving. It happened on two occasions.

On 7 September 2026, Albury Local Court convicted the driver of two counts of making false or misleading work diary entries. The fine was $15,000.

The court called it serious, because the record was made deliberately to get around fatigue laws.

NHVR's advice to operators is to consider electronic work diaries, and to give drivers realistic schedules with break times and places. If a schedule can't be driven legally, a diary won't fix it.
