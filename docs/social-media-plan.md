# TRTT social media and branding: review, decisions and plan

Prepared 30 September 2026. Source data: RobinReach (brand "The Real Transport Toolbox"), the therealtransporttoolbox.vip storefront, and this repository.

## Position

TRTT is the plain-English, primary-source guide to Australian transport law for the people who carry the duty: owner-operators, small fleets, schedulers, loaders, receivers and drivers. The core is free. The industry's answer has been fear. TRTT's answer is a clear reading of the law, a no-blame review of what went wrong, and one action to take on Monday.

The content already does this well. The volume, repeated templates and lack of a quality gate make it look like the noise TRTT wants to cut through.

## Strengths to protect

- The brand voice brief in RobinReach is specific, ESL-aware and anti-fear.
- Posts cite section numbers, name NHVR releases and court registers, add source comments and carry the right disclaimer.
- The "After the Fine" series asks the no-blame question: what made the wrong thing the easy thing.
- Every post ends on an action.
- Free Toolbox Talks lead; paid courses sit in the first comment.
- The best visual is the Runcorn Crossing timeline: dated, plain, sourced, with a striking "0 calls" figure.

## Weaknesses found

1. Placeholder graphics with a literal `n` in place of a line break ("Fatigue ledgernAudit-ready SMS") were published to Instagram and TikTok on 21 to 29 September. Two more were queued for 1 October and have been paused.
2. Up to 10 posts a day, the same copy on several platforms, on a 4-hourly queue, with approval switched off.
3. About two-thirds of queued posts open "Picture this.", "Here's a common one." or "Quick question."
4. Invented characters with specific figures are presented as common cases. Paul Conley's LinkedIn comment treated one as real ("as he had admitted it").
5. Posts break the brand's own rules: "3 things I'd check" lists, em-dash sign-offs, contrast lines, and 300 to 450 word posts against a Year 7 brief.
6. Some imagery reads as generic AI stock (holographic dashboards). The TRTT badge is redrawn inconsistently, and one library image spells it "THE SEAL TRANSPORT TOEHIDE".
7. Three visual identities: navy and mint on social, blue and navy with Saira on the storefront, purple gradient on the old GitHub page.
8. Storefront copy uses hype the brand forbids ("architect of transport certainty", "fortress of legal proof", "The Empire Series"). The certificate threshold reads 80% in one place and 85% in the FAQ. The site's share title is "Audit Ready 2026", not TRTT.
9. The GitHub Pages template contained US rules (ELDs, hours-of-service logs, driver qualification files), unsupported claims and empty testimonials.
10. No link tracking, so posts cannot be tied to downloads or sales.

## Decisions made (30 September 2026)

- **X: reduce.** Kept for news reaction only (target 2 to 3 a week).
- **Queue: pause and thin.** The next 14 days were cut to one main post a day plus videos, carousels and LinkedIn stories. Nothing was deleted.
- **Time-sensitive items go first.** New NHVR notices, advice and court outcomes jump the queue (see the fast lane below).
- **Old GitHub site: replace with a redirect** to therealtransporttoolbox.vip.

## The plan

### News fast lane

TRTT should not be last to the conversation.

- **Sources, checked every weekday by 07:00 AEST:** NHVR news and media releases, NHVR regulatory advice, the NHVR court outcomes register, Fair Work Commission decisions, ATSB reports, state regulators.
- **Target:** post within 24 hours of a notice or advice, same day for a court outcome that affects operators. Two reserved slots a day (08:00 and 12:00 AEST) on LinkedIn and Facebook. X gets the short version.
- **Checklist before publishing:** link the primary source; check every section number and figure against it; state the date; quote penalties exactly; add the no-blame question; add the disclaimer; run the voice check.
- **Rule:** scheduled series wait; the fast lane does not.
- **Automation (not yet set up, needs approval):** a daily check of those sources that saves drafts only and never publishes.

### Content rules

- Show the law: the highlighted section, what it says, what to do on Monday.
- A source line and "last verified" date on every compliance post. Publish visible corrections.
- Label invented scenarios as examples, or use real cases.
- At least one real photo, document or face a week. No holographic dashboards.
- Vary hooks: a scene, a flat fact, a real question.
- Run `python3 tools/voice_lint.py` on every draft before it is scheduled.

### Platform roles

| Platform | Role | Cadence |
|---|---|---|
| LinkedIn | Home base for operators, directors, managers. | 3 to 4 posts a week, up to 250 words, one carousel a week |
| YouTube | Proof engine and the only channel with a measured audience. | 2 a week |
| Facebook | Owner-operators and drivers. One specific question per post. | 3 a week |
| Instagram and TikTok | Re-cuts of the YouTube video only. Never text cards. | Up to 3 a week; review at day 60 |
| X | News reaction only. | 2 to 3 a week |

### Weekly rhythm (8 to 10 unique pieces)

Monday: what changed. Tuesday: video. Wednesday: After the Fine case. Thursday: free Toolbox Talk or checklist. Friday: an operator question, answered the following week. Weekends: at most one evergreen post.

### Engagement

Reply to every human comment within 24 hours (30 minutes a day). Comment thoughtfully on NHVR, ATA and state association posts. Ask specific questions, such as which state took longest to clear a defect.

### Governance

- Switch on post approval in RobinReach settings (not settable through the tools used here).
- 30-minute Sunday review of the next week's queue.
- Add UTM tags to every link.
- Monthly accuracy spot check of the ten most-viewed posts.

### Brand kit (to build)

- One palette: the navy and mint of the social cards, with #002E47 and #015FFB from the storefront as the web pair.
- One type pair, used on both social and web.
- A logo lockup that reads at feed size, with the full "The Real Transport Toolbox" wording redrawn cleanly.
- Four templates built on the Runcorn Crossing slide: timeline, section-highlight, carousel, quote.
- Rewrite the storefront hype copy and fix the 80% versus 85% certificate threshold.

### Presenter and AI avatar policy (proposed)

If AI presenter video is used:
- Say so in the profile bio and on the video, and switch on the platform's AI-generated content label (TikTok, YouTube, Instagram and Facebook all have one).
- Use it for explainers where the script is Herby's. Never for testimonials, "operator" witnesses or case scenes presented as real.
- Get written consent for any real person's likeness or voice.
- Keep real footage and Herby's own voice for trust-critical content: corrections, court cases, announcements.

### Measurement

Baseline first (follower counts, native impressions, link clicks, newsletter signups, Toolbox Talk downloads, course sales). Then track: reply rate within 24 hours, saves and shares per post, real inbound conversations a week, YouTube average views per video (currently about 258), downloads per post. Set numeric targets once the baseline exists.

- **Days 0 to 7:** fix broken assets; reply to comments; verify native insights; switch on approval; set the fast lane in motion; add UTM tags.
- **Days 8 to 30:** brand kit and templates; rebuilt cadence; YouTube video format.
