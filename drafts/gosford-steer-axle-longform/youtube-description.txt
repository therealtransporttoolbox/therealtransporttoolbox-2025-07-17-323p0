TITLE
Steer axle at 134.5%: how a clean-record operator got a severe risk breach at Gosford

DESCRIPTION
A tray truck on the M1 at Mount White, nothing visibly wrong, a company with no prior offences since 2015. Then it was weighed. The steer axle was carrying 8.74 tonnes against a 6.5 tonne limit, 134.5 percent, a severe risk breach under the Heavy Vehicle National Law.

Gosford Local Court, 22 September 2026: conviction and a $21,000 fine, against a maximum of $108,000. The cause in the court record is one sentence: the load was placed too far forward on the tray.

This video covers:
- Why a forward load overloads the steer axle even when the whole truck is under its total mass limit
- The three HVNL mass breach bands and where 134.5 percent lands
- The NHVR's five points for operators, as a Monday morning checklist
- The no-blame question to ask about your own loading
