TITLE
The work diary said resting. The cameras said driving. $15,000 fine #Shorts

DESCRIPTION
In May 2026 NHVR officers stopped a rigid truck towing a trailer at the Twelve Mile Creek Heavy Vehicle Safety Station. The work diary recorded rest. Road safety cameras had sighted the truck travelling, on two occasions.

Albury Local Court, 7 September 2026: two counts of false or misleading work diary entries under HVNL s 325(1). Conviction and a $15,000 fine. The court called them serious examples of deliberately making a false record to get around fatigue laws.

The NHVR's advice to operators: consider electronic work diaries, train drivers, and give them realistic schedules with break times and places. If a schedule cannot be driven legally, a diary will not fix it.
